Match the brief to the house

The house type should narrow the brief. In an older Northcote or Thornbury home, the plan may need to respect existing character and a narrower original floor plan. In a Reservoir brick-veneer home or a newer Mill Park build, the practical question is whether the layout stays put or plumbing and electrical changes enter the job. Before requesting prices, give each contractor the same inputs: suburb or postcode, kitchen size, budget range, desired scope and property constraints. Raise heritage overlay, apartment living or narrow access early, because access and approval constraints can change labour, cartage and sequencing. Those inputs stop the first meeting becoming a guess about whether the job is a kitchen facelift northern suburbs Melbourne update or a full rebuild.

Send every contractor the same brief

Comparable quotes need matching assumptions. Write one brief and send it to each contractor, stating what stays, what goes, whether appliances move, the finish level expected and the contingency you are prepared to hold. Ask each contractor to name the responsible trade for cabinetry, stone, plumbing and electrical work. The reference guide says those tasks should be handled by specialists rather than one generalist covering everything.

  • Ask what is included, what is excluded and who orders materials.
  • Confirm whether demolition is included and when the written scope is finalised.
  • Check how exclusions are described and which choices must be made before ordering begins.
  • Verify VBA registration for the class of work, rather than assuming it from a brand name or advertisement.

Cabinetry and benchtop scope boundaries

Cabinetry and benchtop choices often decide the price band. Custom made kitchens north Melbourne enquiries usually turn on storage and fit: made-to-measure cabinets, drawers and butler's pantries suit a shell that works while joinery wastes space. A kitchen builder north Melbourne brief should separate new cabinetry inside an existing layout from a larger renovation that changes walls, services or appliance positions.

For stone or benchtop installation, treat porcelain, sintered stone and natural stone as a specification conversation. Templating, joins, edge profile, sink type, splashback coordination and appliance clearances can affect the final result. Do not compare benchtop quotes unless removal, preparation and installation are described in the same way.

Common questions

Which service path should be chosen first? Start with what is actually changing, not with a style picture. The six named paths are full renovation, custom cabinetry, stone or benchtop installation, design and planning, facelift, and period-home update. If surfaces, doors or storage are the problem, a facelift or cabinetry path may fit. If workflow, appliance position or bench area is wrong, planning or a full renovation is more realistic.

What should be sent before a contractor quotes? Send the same written brief to each contractor: suburb or postcode, kitchen size, budget range, desired scope and constraints such as heritage overlay, apartment rules or narrow access. State what stays, what goes, whether appliances move, expected finish level and your contingency. Ask who is responsible for cabinetry, stone, plumbing and electrical work, what is excluded, who orders materials, whether demolition is included and when the written scope is finalised. Independent VBA-registered contractors and cabinetmakers are named in the reference guide, so check registration for the class of work.

How can stone benchtop quotes stay comparable? Ask for the same specification in every stone quote: material type, templating, joins, edge profile, sink type, splashback coordination, appliance clearances, removal, preparation and installation. Porcelain, sintered stone and natural stone should not be treated as interchangeable. If one quote omits preparation or removal, the lower figure may not be comparable. A written quote should reflect the actual site, chosen materials and trade scope, not only a calculator range.
